# Liked posts (swipe file)

Posts by **other people** whose voice, structure, or hook Richard wants to learn from. This is a swipe file — not for copying, for studying. When the voice guide gets refined, these show what "good" looks like *as a target*, while Richard's own posts define what he *actually* sounds like.

> Keep the two separate in your head: **Richard's posts = his voice. Liked posts = aspiration/structure to learn from.** Never let a liked post's voice overwrite Richard's own.

---

## How to add one

1. (Optional) Screenshot into this folder: `liked-<author>-<topic>.png`.
2. Add an entry below using the template.
3. **Transcribe the text** so it's usable.
4. Note *specifically what you like* — the hook? the structure? the honesty? That note is the whole point.
