**Story in one line:** a Mexican bank needed people who distrust banks to use ATMs instead of tellers; the studio redesigned the ATM itself, hardware and interface, tested with users through AR.

**Meta:** Client Banco Azteca · Mexico · Banking · 2019. Tags: banking, experience design, digital platforms, universal design.

## What sells

- **Specific users, specific fear.** Not "improve UX": people afraid of losing money or breaking the machine.
- **Method with counts.** 3,000 km, 100+ users, hundreds of prototypes.
- **The timeline carries the whole story** in four dated lines, with the numbers in the last one.
- **The research-wall photo.** Effort you can see.
- **Interface flow drawn as 3 steps**, not shown as screens. A diagram where a screen would say less.

## What's weak

- No "before" ATM. The fear is described, never shown.
- AR is in the title of the solution but no AR image appears.

## Steal this

- **Four-date timeline** that retells the case with results at the end.
- **Research evidence + clean result** as a pair.
- **Flow as a simple step diagram** instead of a screen dump.
