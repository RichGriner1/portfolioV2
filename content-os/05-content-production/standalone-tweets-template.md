# Standalone Tweets Template

Single sharp observations. One opinion, one lesson, or one one-liner per tweet — each able to stand completely on its own. These are the offcuts and distillations of a story: the sentences too good to bury in a thread.

**Source material:** processed Granola transcript (especially the "Keep verbatim" and "Gold" lists) + Story Bank entry + voice guide.

## Proven structures (from the swipe file)

Studied from creators like Matt Gray (`02-voice-guide/examples/liked-posts.md`) — borrow the **shape**, fill it with Richard's lived design/AI specifics, never the guru voice:

- **Triad / parallelism:** three parallel clauses that build. *"First design system: you name the colors. Second: you name the roles. Third: you let the tokens decide."*
- **List-post + kicker:** a titled list, then one punchy closing line. *"3 things I check before any handoff: tokens match code · states defined · the grid is real. Miss one and the devs design it for you."*
- **"N [things] of [X]" frame:** *"The 3 mistakes I made naming tokens…"*
- **One-line aphorism — earned, not preached:** only if it comes from a real moment, not a maxim.

## Rules for this template

- **Each tweet stands fully alone.** No setup, no context needed. If it needs the story to make sense, it belongs in a thread, not here.
- **One idea per tweet.** Opinion, lesson, or observation — pick one.
- **Sharp over safe.** These live or die on having an actual point of view.
- **Mine the verbatim list.** The best standalone tweets are often things you already said out loud in Granola — barely edited.
- **No threads disguised as tweets.** No "1/", no "more below."
- **Variety.** Mix opinions, lessons, and reframes so they don't all sound the same.
- These are also great raw material for the weekly cadence between bigger pieces — a story can keep producing standalone tweets long after the blog ships.
