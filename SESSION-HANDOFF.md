## Goal

Land freelance **design-system consulting** clients (AI + fintech startups, seed–Series C, North America) via personalized cold outreach — email + LinkedIn/X. Capacity-bound side practice fronted by Richard's personal brand, with a trusted collective as the build bench. Full-time job stays.

## Offer ladder

| Tier | Offer | Price | Who delivers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | **Design System Audit** — UI-drift map, token/component gap analysis, design→code parity, 90-day roadmap, quick wins | **$3k** flat, ~1 wk | Richard, solo |
| Mid | **Core System Build** — token architecture + 30–60 components + design→eng handoff + docs | **$12–25k** fixed | Richard leads + collective |
| High | Full system + governance | $30–60k | Collective (Series B/C only) |
| Retainer | Design-system steward | $3–6k/mo | Sold at build close, bills after handoff |

**Audit workflow:** Day 1 kickoff (30 min — get Figma + product login + ideally read-only frontend repo) → Days 2–5 solo async teardown → Day 7 live walkthrough (the roadmap IS the build pitch). Parity without repo = DevTools computed styles vs. Figma.

## Numbers

6 builds/year ← ~12 paid audits/year (~1/month) ← ~25–35 sales calls ← **~15–25 personalized touches/week**. Scale outreach, not audit count.
