## The goal

Land freelance **design-system consulting** clients (AI + fintech startups, seed–Series C, North America) via personalized cold outreach — email + LinkedIn/X DMs — without paid subscriptions. Richard has a full-time job, so this is a capacity-bound side practice fronted by his personal brand, with a small trusted collective as the build bench.

## The offer ladder

| Tier | Offer | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | **Design System Audit** — UI-drift map, token/component gap analysis, design→code parity, 90-day roadmap, quick wins | **$3k** flat (~1 wk) | Cold-outreach offer. Richard solo, always. |
| Mid | **Core System Build** — token architecture + 30–60 components + design→eng handoff + docs | **$12–25k** fixed | The revenue engine. The audit's roadmap = its scope. Delivered by the collective. |
| High | Full system + governance | $30–60k | Series B/C only. Don't lead with it. |
| Retainer | Design system steward | $3–6k/mo | Sold at build close (build + 3-mo starter bundle), bills after handoff. |

**Audit workflow:** Day 1 kickoff (30 min, get Figma + product login + ideally read-only frontend repo) → Days 2–5 solo async teardown → Day 7 live walkthrough (the roadmap IS the build pitch). Parity without repo = DevTools computed styles vs. Figma.

## The numbers

Capacity target: **6 builds/year** → needs **~12 paid audits/year (~1/month)** → ~25–35 sales calls → **~15–25 personalized outreach touches/week**. Do NOT chase audit volume (4/mo = too much). Scale *outreach*, let audits be the outcome. Win rate needed: ~0.3% of a 1,500–2,500-company pond.

## Positioning: personal front, collective bench

- All outreach, personal brand (Twitter), and invoices come from **Richard** — founders reply to a person.
- Proof, for now, is individual (his in-house work). The collective stays invisible until it has 2–3 delivered builds + case studies as a group.
- The line: **"I lead; I bring specialists when the scope calls for it."** On the landing page + invoice only, kept soft.
- The design-system **build** is the collective's first real paid-as-a-group project. See the private Collective artifact for members, operating model, and split framework.
