# Blog Template

Long-form. Enough room to fully develop one story and its lesson — no longer. Story-first, practical, opinionated. A practitioner sharing what actually happened.

**Source material:** processed Granola transcript + Story Bank entry + research brief (if any) + voice guide.

---

## Structure

```markdown
---
title:
pillar:            # Case Study | Product Case Study | Blog | Passion Project | The Collective
story_id:          # link to Story Bank entry
status: draft      # draft | edited | ready | shipped
target_date:
formats_spun_off:  # LinkedIn / X thread / tweets produced from this
---

# [Title — concrete and specific, not clickbait]

## The hook (open with the moment)
[Start inside a real scene. The specific thing that happened. No throat-clearing, no "in today's world." Drop the reader straight into the story. 1–3 short paragraphs.]

## What happened
[Tell the story. The sequence. The turning point. Use real names, numbers, projects (KT360, AFI, etc.). Preserve verbatim phrases from the transcript where they're strong.]

## The lesson (the why)
[Now surface the reasoning. Why did this matter? What did it change? This is where your opinion lives — state it plainly. Show the thinking, don't just assert the conclusion.]

## The counterpoint (optional but strong)
[Address the obvious "yeah but." Honesty here builds trust. If research surfaced a tension, this is where it goes.]

## What to take from it
[The practical takeaway. What the reader can now do or see differently. Specific, not "leverage these learnings." If a framework genuinely emerged from repeated experience, name it here — but only if it's real.]

## Close
[Land the point. End on the insight or a genuine open question. No generic CTA.]
```

---

## Rules for this template

- **Open with the story, never the thesis.** The point should emerge, not lead.
- **One story, one lesson.** If you're covering three lessons, that's three blogs.
- **Keep verbatim phrases** from the transcript — they carry the voice.
- **Subheads are signposts**, not SEO bait. They should make the piece skimmable and honest.
- **Length follows the story.** Long enough to develop it fully; cut everything else.
- **Research supports, never leads.** At most a line or two of outside evidence, in service of the story.
- Run the finished draft through `editing-prompt.md` and the voice guide before it's "ready."
