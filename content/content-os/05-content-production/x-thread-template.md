# X Thread Template

One story or one argument, told step by step. The first tweet is the whole game — it must stand alone and earn the read. No thread-bait.

**Source material:** processed Granola transcript + Story Bank entry + voice guide.

---

## Structure

```markdown

---

# Thread: [working title — not posted]

**1/ [THE HOOK]**
[Stands completely alone. A real moment or a sharp claim. If someone reads only this tweet, it still lands. No "a thread 🧵" with nothing behind it — the hook IS the promise. ≤280 chars.]

**2/ [SET THE SCENE]**
[The specific situation. Project, stakes, what was at risk. Concrete.]

**3/ [WHAT HAPPENED — part 1]**
[The story moves. One beat per tweet. Keep each tweet able to be read fast.]

**4/ [WHAT HAPPENED — the turning point]**
[The moment it changed. This is often the most screenshot-able tweet — make it land.]

**5/ [THE REASONING]**
[Why it mattered. Your opinion, stated plainly. The thinking, not just the conclusion.]

**6/ [THE TAKEAWAY]**
[What the reader can use. Practical and specific.]

**7/ [CLOSE]**
[Land the point. A clean final line, or one genuine question. No "follow me for more" begging. A simple "that's it" or the sharpest restatement of the insight works.]
```

---

## Rules for this template

- **Tweet 1 must stand alone.** Write it so a reader who never expands the thread still got something. That's also what makes them expand it.
- **One beat per tweet.** Each tweet is a single, fast-to-read step.
- **One argument or one story per thread.** Don't braid two ideas.
- **Build to a screenshot-able line** — the turning point or the takeaway. Make at least one tweet quotable on its own.
- **Keep verbatim phrases** from the transcript. Your real wording reads as human on X, which is exactly the edge.
- **Number tweets** (1/, 2/…) so the structure is clear while drafting.
- **No thread-bait, no follow-farming.** The content earns the follow.
- Length: as many tweets as the story needs, usually 5–9. Cut filler tweets ruthlessly.
