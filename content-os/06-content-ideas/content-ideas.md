# Content Ideas

The running master list of every content idea, organized by type. This is **not** a content calendar — it's a reservoir. The weekly prioritization step cross-references chosen stories against this list to see what a single lived experience can unlock.

**How to use it:**
- Add ideas freely as they come up (from Wispr, Granola, or just thinking).
- An idea here is a *topic*, not a *story*. It only becomes content when a real Story Bank entry backs it. **No story → no draft** (rule #8).
- When the weekly review connects a story to one of these, mark it: `→ [story-id]`.
- When a piece ships, mark it `✅ shipped` and link the backlog entry.

> Stories come first. These ideas are prompts for memory — "have I lived something that speaks to this?" — not assignments to go research and generate.
