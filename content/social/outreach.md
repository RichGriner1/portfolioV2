# Outreach playbook

Growth needs engagement, not just posting — especially for a small account. This is the system so outreach doesn't become a daily "who do I even talk to?" decision. Calendar block: **Wednesdays 18:00–19:00** (see `content/social/README.md`). Target list: [`outreach-targets.md`](outreach-targets.md).

## The rule

**X is primary** (open reply culture, faster ROI). LinkedIn is secondary: thoughtful comments on design/AI-fintech posts, plus **warm DMs** *(rule change 2026-08-03 — was comment-only)*. A DM is fine when there's a real reason — you've already engaged with them publicly, you're citing their work, or you have a specific question only they can answer. No cold pitches, no freelance asks: a salesy DM from a small account burns the contact. DMs are private, so the boss-visibility concern lives where it always did — **public** post copy (the "no DM me / available for projects" rule in `voice.md` still holds). Full-time job → this is *professional networking*, not selling.

## The session (~1 hr, once/week)

Work the tiers top-down from the target list:

| Tier | Who | The move | Count | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **10k+** | big design/AI/fintech voices | 1–2 sharp value-add replies or quote-tweets | 1–2 | borrow their audience |
| **1k–10k** | peers, adjacents | genuine replies; a warm DM only when there's a real reason | 3–5 | relationships + reciprocity |
| **0–1k** | your level, emerging | follow / support / reply | rest of the hour | community that grows with you |

**What "value-add" means:** a reply that teaches or extends the point (a specific example, a counter-case, a number) — not "great post 🔥". The reply *is* the portfolio. Expert reply → profile visit → your pinned link.

## Conversion path

engage → profile visit → pinned tweet / bio link → portfolio → (later) meeting CTA.
The bio/pinned link is doing the selling, so the replies just need to be smart and consistent.

## Sourcing targets (how the list gets built)

- **From capture (warm):** people/tools already in Richard's world — content references, chats, tools he uses. Reliable; Claude maintains this.
- **From the space (cold-ish):** known design-system / AI / fintech accounts.
- **From scrolling (Richard):** fresh accounts found in-app — the part Claude can't do live. Add them to the list with a tier.

Claude can't browse X/LinkedIn live or confirm exact follower counts, so **tiers are best-guess — confirm in-app.**

## Tracking

Log lightly in `outreach-targets.md`: who you engaged, when, and whether it landed (reply back / follow / profile visit). Over a few weeks the pattern shows which tier and which accounts actually convert — then weight the hour toward those.

## Weekly upkeep

During the Friday content review, Claude refreshes the target list (new names from the week's Granola/chats) and flags 3–5 accounts worth hitting the following Wednesday.
