## What sells

- **Device spread right after the word "omnichannel".** Watch + TV says it faster than the paragraph.
- **Icon grid as the design-system proof.** One sheet shows the visual code.
- **Principles with a concrete example.** "Call to pro-action" is backed by deferred payment and month prediction.
- **Brand moments as section breaks.** Black logo slide before the concept.

## What's weak

- **No numbers, no timeline, no outcome.** We don't learn if young users came back.
- The two challenges restate the brief.
- "Consistency and meaning" closes on adjectives (flexible, modular, scalable) instead of a result.

## Steal this

- **Device spread** for any multi-surface or white-label story (our simulators across banks).
- **Icon grid / system sheet** as one-image proof of a visual system.
- **Principle + example** pairs.
- **Brand moment** as a section break in a visual-identity case study.
