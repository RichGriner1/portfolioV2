> Keep the two separate in your head: **Richard's posts = his voice. Liked posts = aspiration/structure to learn from.** Never let a liked post's voice overwrite Richard's own.

## How to add one

1. (Optional) Screenshot into this folder: `liked-<author>-<topic>.png`.
2. Add an entry below using the template.
3. **Transcribe the text** so it's usable.
4. Note *specifically what you like* — the hook? the structure? the honesty? That note is the whole point.

### Matt Gray (@matt_gray_) — LinkedIn "N rare traits" numbered post

- **Platform:** LinkedIn
- **Images:** `liked-mattgray-linkedin-8-traits-1.png` … `-4.png` (a 4-screenshot post)
- **What I like about it:** The canonical long-form LinkedIn structure. A credential hook, then N numbered items, each with a bulleted sub-list and a one-line **contrast kicker**. Massive white space — one idea per line. Skims in five seconds, rewards a full read. This is the model behind `05-content-production/linkedin-post-template.md`.
- **What to borrow (STRUCTURE, not voice):**
  - **Credential + number hook:** *"I've hired 90+ high performers in the last 3 years. Here are 8 rare traits I see in every high performer:"* — proof + a promised count.
  - **Per-item pattern:** `N. Title Case Name` → a one-line framing → a `What is it?` / `If they:` lead-in → a 2–3 item bullet sub-list → a **contrast kicker** (*"Hard work is common. Grit is rare."*).
  - **White space is the formatting.** Every sentence on its own line.
  - **A handwritten-notes photo** as the attached image — his signature visual.
  - Richard's version fills each item with a real design/AI/fintech lesson (token tiers, the 90-minute prototype, the PrimeNG audit), never a hiring platitude.
- **⛔ What NOT to borrow:**
  - The **CTA funnel:** *"♻️ Repost it to your network and follow Matt Gray for more"* + *"Get the complete framework here: [lead-magnet link]."* Richard has a full-time job — **soft CTAs only** (decided this convo). No repost-bait, no "follow for more", no lead magnet, no ♻️ funnel marker.
  - The **content** is generic hiring-guru advice ("Grit", "Optimism", "Not Being an Asshole"). Borrow the *shape*, not the maxims.

**Transcription:**

> I've hired 90+ high performers in the last 3 years.
>
> Here are 8 rare traits I see in every high performer:
>
> **1. Grit** — This one's a non-negotiable. What is it? • Ability to persist through obstacles • Strength to bounce back from failure • Tenacity to keep pushing, no matter the challenge. Hard work is common. Grit is rare.
>
> **2. Relentless Resourcefulness** — Some people find a way no matter how "impossible" it seems. Innovative problem solvers. The conviction that there's always a solution. They're like water. They find a path around any obstacle.
>
> **3. Optimism** — Entrepreneurship is a roller coaster. Optimistic people don't see roadblocks; they see stepping stones. Their energy is electrifying and infectious. A must-have when the going gets tough.
>
> **4. Constant Learning** — The ROI on constant learners compounds. These hires: • Seek feedback • Expand their skills • Stay curious and hungry. Train them well, and they'll give you an infinite return.
>
> **5. Not Being an Asshole** — It might sound simple, but it's vital. I don't care how smart, hardworking, or optimistic you are. If you don't • Show empathy • Collaborate well • Treat others with respect — I don't want you on my team. Period.
>
> **6. Fun to Be Around** — Work can be intense. People who bring • Positive energy • A sense of humor • A lightness to the office make a world of difference. When we make work feel like play, we're unstoppable.
>
> **7. Growth Mindset** — This one is foundational. Someone who • Embraces challenges as growth opportunities • Believes abilities and intelligence can be developed. They get obsessed with the process. Not fixated on the outcome.
>
> **8. Enjoys Responsibility** — I need my team to take on responsibility. I don't want to chase people down. If they • Take ownership of their work • Care about doing a good job — we'll perform better. And I'll have way more breathing room.
>
