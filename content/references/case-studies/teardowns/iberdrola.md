[Live page (EN)](https://www.garajedeideas.com/projects/iberdrola) · [ES](https://www.garajedeideas.com/es/projects/iberdrola)

**Story in one line:** product and content design across an energy company's digital products in five countries, built on a shared design system with four principles.

**Meta:** Industry: utilities · Services: product design, content design, management · Timeline: 2022–present.

**Template:** Garaje, **product-by-product** variant. Closest to how a multi-product design-system case study of ours would read.

## What sells

- **Product-by-product sections.** Six products, one heading and one paragraph each. Scannable, and it shows breadth.
- **Principle cards.** Four named principles, each with a one-line definition and an image. The design system as something you can see.
- **"Laying the foundations" year card.** The first year framed as groundwork, so later years read as growth.
- **Values stacked as words** next to a product card.

## What's weak

- **No numbers at all.** Not even the number of screens or components.
- The design system is mentioned twice (public web, principles) but never shown beyond the four cards.
- Six product paragraphs with almost no matching screens.

## Steal this

- **Product sections** for a DS case study: one block per product that runs on the system.
- **Principle cards** for our design principles.
- **Year card** to open an evolution section.
- **Add what they skipped:** a screen per product, and a count.
