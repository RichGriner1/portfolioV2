# Writing substance (the first gate)

**Substance is the first gate. Tone is the second.** A piece that fails here doesn't get a voice pass — it gets cut or rebuilt. This skill checks whether a piece is *worth reading and clear*, independent of whose voice it's in or what language it's in.

Distilled from `content/voice.md` (portfolioV2), generalized to any prose. It targets the recurring failure: **things that are unclear, too short, or forced** — copy that passes a tone lint and still isn't good writing.

Two references carry the detail:
- [substance-bar.md](references/substance-bar.md) — is it worth reading?
- [clarity-and-length.md](references/clarity-and-length.md) — does it parse, and is it developed enough?

## The gate, in one pass
1. **One takeaway, stated.** Can you say the point in one sentence? If *you* can't quote it back from the text, it isn't ready → revise. Don't make the reader infer it.
2. **A real "so what," on the page.** The reason it matters is named in words, not left implied by a story.
3. **Non-obvious.** If a competent peer already knows it, go deeper or cut it. ("Here's my workflow" is not automatically valuable.)
4. **Clear.** Every sentence parses on first read. One idea per sentence. Concrete before abstract.
5. **Developed enough.** Long enough to fully develop the one point — no longer. Too short = the point is asserted, not earned. (See length notes.)
6. **Not forced.** No angle, reframe, or symmetry that only exists to sound punchy. If it needs the construction to land, cut it.

## The three tests (apply in order)
- **The "and?" test** — read it and ask "…and?" If there's no answer on the page, the so-what is missing.
- **The screenshot test** — would a practitioner screenshot this to keep or send a coworker?
- **The subtraction test** — if deleting the piece would cost the reader nothing, don't ship it.

## This skill is neutral — layer voice/language on top
- It does **not** judge tone, banned phrases, or whether it "sounds like Richard" — that's `voice-griner` (English) or the house-style skill.
- It does **not** judge language quality — Spanish gets `afi-redaccion` (Peninsular, calque-hunt) layered on top.
- `/content-review` composes: `writing-substance` (this) + the right language lens. A piece must clear *this* gate first; a beautifully-voiced post with no takeaway still fails.
