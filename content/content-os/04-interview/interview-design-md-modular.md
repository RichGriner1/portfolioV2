## Set the scene

1. Take me back to the Design MD showcase you built in 3–4 hours. What were you actually trying to prove to yourself — or to the team — when you started it?

2. Describe what the thing *was*, in plain terms. Three markdown layers — Content, Design, Agents. What lived in each one, and why those three?

## What happened — the experiment

3. **(The key one — don't rush this.)** You ran it with the Design MD and without it. Pick the single clearest example: what did the AI spit out *without* the Design MD that was wrong or off? You mentioned header background tokens, table lines, and tags needing work — walk me through one of those. Then: what changed when the Design MD was in?
   - *Follow-up:* If you showed both outputs to someone side by side, what's the first difference they'd notice?

4. Then you tested it on an existing project vs. a new one. What was different about how much the Design MD actually mattered in each case?

## The reasoning (the why)

5. Here's the part most people miss: on an *existing* project, you found the AI barely needs the Design MD. In your own words — why? What is the AI actually doing instead of reading your doc?
   - *Follow-up:* Does that hold on a *messy* codebase too, or only a clean one? Be honest about the edge.

6. You moved from one big Design MD document to a "skills + router" setup — a base, plus attachable skills like buttons and drawers. What pushed you there? What was annoying or breaking with the single doc?

7. You called it "a changelog, never finished." What do you mean by that — how is it different from how people normally think about documentation?

## The stance + the counterpoint

8. So if someone's about to write a big design.md for their team right now — what would you actually tell them? When is it worth it, and when is it documenting into the void?

9. Push back on yourself: when does a Design MD *still* earn its keep on an old codebase? Where would you be wrong to say "don't bother"?

## Stakes + the number

10. What did this whole approach actually save you — hours, AI spend, back-and-forth with devs? Give me the realest number or before/after you've got.

11. The "Caveman" repo — what is it, what does it do, and how much did it cut your token cost?
