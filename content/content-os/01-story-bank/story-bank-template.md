# Story Bank Template

This is the structure for every Story Bank entry. The Story Bank is the heart of Content OS — a running log of lived experiences, lessons, and moments that *could* become content. Most entries never become a post, and that's fine. The job is to capture, not to publish.

**How it works:** the daily processing prompt reads your Wispr notes and appends new entries here using this exact shape. Keep the newest entries at the top. Never delete — archive instead.

---

## Field guide

- **ID** — short slug + date, e.g. `afi-wealth-onboarding-2026-06-21`. Used for cross-referencing.
- **Date** — when the experience happened (not when it was logged, if different).
- **Pillar / Type** — which content bucket this maps to (Case Study, Product Case Study, Blog, Passion Project, The Collective). Cross-reference with `06-content-ideas/content-ideas.md`.
- **The story (what happened)** — the actual moment, in plain terms. Specific. A real thing that occurred.
- **The lesson / insight** — what you took from it. The reasoning. The opinion.
- **Why it matters** — who it helps and why anyone should care.
- **Strength** — `seed` (rough, needs more) / `solid` (has a real story) / `strong` (clear story + stakes + opinion, ready to produce).
- **Connected ideas** — links to entries in `content-ideas.md` or other Story Bank IDs that share a thread. This is how frameworks get discovered.
- **Useful phrases** — exact wording from Wispr/Granola worth preserving verbatim.
- **Open questions** — what's missing; what to ask in an interview; what to research.
- **Status** — `captured` / `prioritized` / `in-research` / `interviewed` / `drafted` / `shipped`.

## Example entry (for reference)

### [ID: ai-feedback-dynamic-2026-06-18]

- **Date:** 2026-06-18
- **Pillar / Type:** Blog — "AI changes the feedback dynamic for designers"
- **The story (what happened):** On the AFI Wealth Manager project, I started using AI to draft the *reasoning* behind a component decision before review, instead of just presenting the design. The stakeholder meeting went completely differently — we argued about the logic, not the pixels.
- **The lesson / insight:** AI doesn't just speed up production. It shifts what feedback is *about*. When the reasoning is documented up front, reviews stop being taste fights and become decisions about logic.
- **Why it matters:** Designers (especially non-technical ones shipping real products) spend huge energy defending choices. This reframes the whole review dynamic.
- **Strength:** strong
- **Connected ideas:** "AI allows designers to explain reasoning and document decisions", "Revision is the most critical moment now"
- **Useful phrases:**
  - "We argued about the logic, not the pixels."
- **Open questions:** Need the actual before/after on the AFI review — how many revision cycles did it cut? Worth a Granola walk-through.
- **Status:** captured
