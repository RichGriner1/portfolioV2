# Tools & the market — companies that grow their scope

Standalone-only. Source tag: **BRK**. Observations on tools/companies expanding past their original identity to follow where their users are heading. Grants the complaint (nostalgia is real) before making the point — critic, not cynic.

Skeleton legend: §17 one-sentence opener + analogy.

---

**Standalone A — Figma follows the market** *(§17 + analogy)* — ✅ LI

"This isn't the Figma I signed up for" — designers, watching Slides, Make, and code all get added to Figma.

Amazon didn't stay a bookstore. They watched where the trend was going — people wanted anything delivered to their door, not just books — and followed it. Figma saw design moving into motion and code, and moved with it. A tool that refuses to grow its scope is choosing to age out.
