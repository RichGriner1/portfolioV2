## Twitter — five standalones (one every 3 to 4 days, not a thread)

**Tweet 1 — the tutorial (Josh)** (280 chars)

showed my old CEO the new portfolio. he's not a designer. he opened the canvas and had no idea what to do.

so I added a tutorial that shows anyone new to a canvas how to move around, and capped the zoom so the sections stay on screen.

keep the concept, let anyone learn it fast.

_Demo:_ 5 s clip: land on the canvas, zoom all the way out (neighbouring sections stay in view), the first tutorial chip appears next to the cursor.

---

**Tweet 2 — personality in a serious product** (273 chars)

a b2b fintech product can't be flashy. nobody wants a flourish between them and a number.

so the personality goes into the small stuff. the theme toggle, the language switch, a checkbox that draws its own tick.

not must-haves. they just make the boring parts less boring.

_Demo:_ 5 s clip: flip the theme toggle, then the EN/ES switch, on a case-study frame. Maybe the Wealth Planner checkbox drawing its tick if a clip exists.

**Tweet 3 — the park (Wispr Flow)** (249 chars)

I'd always start a new portfolio and just stare at a blank page.

I was always tired of just looking at a screen, so I started this one at a park. I opened wispr flow and talked about how I design, what I've built, what I think. With zero structure.

Then I brought it into claude code, but I had already gained excitement from working on it, and being in nature improved my mood.

**Tweet 4 — format is positioning** (269 chars)

I was talking to my friend who is a savage at branding and works at a design consultancy. He said my more traditional portfolio was very AI workflow, so I'd be applying for technical jobs and more likely to get them.

My challenge is that I don't come from engineering.

**Reply** (181 chars)

We needed to show something for design. So I thought of the figma concept as the UI, a look and feel away from the traditional scroll, and added other case studies that are more UX.

_Demo:_ 20 to 30 s tour: land, zoom out, drag across sections, open a case study, close it, flip theme. The hero demo. Square frames, solid fill, round corners in CSS.

**Tweet 5 — the tutorial you can reopen (post after the feature ships)** (244 chars)

My friend said the canvas tutorial might go by too fast. Fair, each step only lasts five seconds if you don't do the move.

So now the four moves just sit in the corner. Dim until you hover, then they come forward. Close it and it stays closed.

_Demo:_ 5 s clip: legend sitting dim in the corner, cursor moves onto it and it comes to full opacity, then the x collapses it to the ?.
