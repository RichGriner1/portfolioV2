# Backlog

Where finished, manually-edited content lands — scheduled and ready to ship. A piece only enters the backlog after you've done your manual edit pass and set `status: ready`.

This is the end of the pipeline. The backlog is the proof the system works: a stockpile of real, story-first content ready to go out on a steady cadence.

**Flow in:** drafting → editing prompt → your manual pass → here.
**Flow out:** you copy/paste into LinkedIn / X / your blog when the date comes, then mark it `shipped`.

---

## How to use

- One entry per piece. Group a blog and its spun-off LinkedIn/X/tweets under the same story so you can stagger them.
- Set a `target_date` and a `platform`. Aim for a sustainable cadence (e.g. 2–3 posts/week).
- After posting, update `status: shipped` and (optionally) note how it did.
- Keep ~2 weeks of `ready` content in here at all times — that's the buffer the weekly loop refills.

## Cadence target

- **Blog:** ~1 / week (or every other week — they're the anchor).
- **LinkedIn:** 2–3 / week.
- **X threads:** 1–2 / week.
- **Standalone tweets:** daily-ish filler between bigger pieces — they cost the least and keep presence steady.

Adjust to what's sustainable. A backlog that's always ~2 weeks deep means a bad week never breaks the streak.
