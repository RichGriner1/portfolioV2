## What sells

- The results ticker under the title. You know it worked before you know what it was.
- Stat mosaic in the solution section: the brand values arrive as numbers a buyer cares about (subsidies, savings, sunny days).
- The marquee word followed by three phones. One word, then the proof.
- Metrics are captioned against a baseline ("compared with the original business plan").

## What's weak

- The page never shows the landing page it started from as a "before". The hero laptop may be it, but nothing says so.
- Challenge text is market generalities (offers look alike) rather than a concrete before-state.
- The joint-venture context (Repsol + Movistar) arrives at step 14. It would sharpen the opening.

## Steal this

- **Results ticker** under the title.
- **Stat mosaic** to make abstract values concrete.
- **Marquee word → screen row** as a section break.
