## What sells

- **Growth numbers stand in for outcome numbers.** 7 → 27 people, 5+ platforms, 5× workload. When you can't share business metrics, count the scope.
- **The bubble diagram.** The only true diagram in the six. It turns "we handled many technologies" into something you can see.
- **Challenges named and numbered**, each with a concrete noun (Angular, Ionic, Drupal).
- **Phases** give a long engagement a shape.

## What's weak

- Almost no product: one login form and one website. A reader can't judge the design quality.
- Results repeat the problem context. The page says 7 → 27 three times.
- Mood photos (bedroom, baby) don't connect to the text next to them except as "health insurance".

## Steal this

- **Scope growth as the metric** for long engagements (Afi: products on the DS, people using it).
- **Concept diagram for the challenge** section.
- **Phases 1–3** to structure a multi-year story.
- **Metric chips on a photo** when there are several small results.
