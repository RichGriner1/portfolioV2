# IKEA — Garaje de Ideas

[Live page (EN)](https://www.garajedeideas.com/projects/ikea) · [ES](https://www.garajedeideas.com/es/projects/ikea)

**Story in one line:** five years embedded with IKEA's design, business and tech teams, starting with B2B (IKEA for Business) and widening to consumer offers.

**Meta:** Industry: retail · Services: service design, design research, product design, content design · Timeline: 5+ years.

**Template:** Garaje. A **partnership** case study like Adeslas, but with more brand and less proof.

## What sells

- **The brand carries the page.** Blue and yellow blocks, the wordmark, product photos. A big client lends credibility.
- **Giant years** as a results graphic: time is the proof.
- **Closing sentence with inline images**: a strong last beat.

## What's weak

- **No numbers.** "Improving conversion" with no figure. "25+ countries" is the only count.
- Takeaways are about the studio ("we embed", "we're multidisciplinary"), not the outcome.
- Product appears in two small phone screens. You can't tell what was designed.
- Graphics are mostly brand assets the client made, not work the studio did.

## Steal this

- **Inline images in a closing sentence.**
- **Big years** when duration is the story.
- **Don't steal:** a page carried by the client's brand. Our clients are smaller and often confidential; the work has to carry it.
