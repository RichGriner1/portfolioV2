# Rows — rows.gg landing page

Live page: https://rows.gg · Screenshots in [rows-gg/](rows-gg/), taken 2026-11-27.

**Story in one line:** a shared list for tracking anything, sold one behaviour at a time.

**Not a case study.** It's a product landing page. We keep it for its **structure and pace**, not its content: it explains a product in short, separate beats that a skimmer can't get lost in.

**Template:** hero + a column of beats. Each beat is one small live visual on the left and a short headline plus two short paragraphs on the right, with a lot of air between beats.

## What sells

- **One idea per beat.** Every headline is a full, plain sentence that makes one claim. Skim the headlines alone and you have the story.
- **The visual is small and specific.** A grey card with two or three rows, cropped to the behaviour. Never a full screen. You see the thing the text is about, and nothing else.
- **Text beside, not under.** Visual left, words right, same height. The eye goes picture → headline → two short paragraphs, then a long gap before the next beat.
- **Short paragraphs.** Two paragraphs of about 30 to 40 words each. The second one usually grants a trade-off or adds a dry aside ("The funeral is purely cosmetic").
- **The demo is the first action.** «Enjoy the demo» sits next to the main call to action in the hero and again at the end.
- **The same visual language in every beat** (grey card, white rows, coloured priority dot, status pill), so the page reads as one thing.

## What's weak

- **No proof.** No numbers, no users, no outcome. It sells feel, which suits a landing page and not a case study.
- **No "why".** We never learn what problem led to each decision. A case study needs the before.
- **Light on context.** Who it's for is never said.

## Steal this

- **Hero with two actions:** "Enjoy the demo" and "Read the story". The demo is the proof; the story is for whoever wants the why.
- **Beats, not sections:** one claim per beat, as a headline sentence. Small visual left, two short paragraphs right.
- **Crop to the behaviour.** Show the two rows that changed, not the screen they live in.
- **Keep the case-study spine underneath** (before → problem → decisions → result) from [anatomy.md](../anatomy.md). Rows has the pace; the studios have the substance.
